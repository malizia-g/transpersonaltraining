Where we gather

Our Venues

Inspiring settings for our residential intensives — the Black Forest, the hills of Tuscany, and a new home for our Eastern track

Home Training Venues

The online lectures bring our whole international community together, but the residential intensives are held separately for each track. Our Western-track intensives take place at two very different but equally inspiring locations in Western Europe, while a dedicated venue for the Eastern (Russian-language) track is being finalised. Each offers quiet, natural surroundings, shared living spaces and room for deep experiential work, rest and community.

Todtmoos · Black Forest, Germany

A house in the Black Forest

Our German intensives are held in a welcoming house nestled in the forested hills of Todtmoos, in the southern Black Forest. Surrounded by woodland and birdsong, it is a place designed to support deep work, rest and connection.

A light-filled training hall, shared living and dining spaces, a sunlit terrace and a garden give the group everything it needs to settle in and turn inward for the duration of the intensive.

Tenuta di Monteverdi · Tuscany, Italy

A Tuscan estate in the Maremma

Our Italian intensives take place at the Tenuta di Monteverdi, a historic estate of over 1,000 hectares in the Maremma, in southern Tuscany. It is centred on a restored 15th-century hamlet with an 18th-century church, wrapped in olive groves, vineyards, woodland and rolling hills.

Renovated farmhouses, elegant common rooms, gardens and a pool provide a serene and beautiful setting for residential intensives — with Siena, Montalcino and Florence within easy reach for those who wish to explore.

Estate photos courtesy of grimaldisavelli.com.

Eastern track · Eastern Europe

A home for our Eastern intensives

Students on our Russian-language Eastern track share the same online lectures as the rest of the community, but gather for their residential intensives at their own dedicated venue.

We are currently finalising this location for the 2027–2030 cohort. Details, photographs and directions will appear here soon — in the meantime, please get in touch if you would like to know more about the Eastern track.

Closer to home

Secondary & collaborative venues

Alongside our main residential intensives, we also run smaller seminars together with our own teachers and other facilitators who collaborate with the school. Held at their own venues, closer to where our community lives, these gatherings make it possible — especially for Holotropic Breathwork and other healing practices — to build up the practice hours you need without having to travel too far.

Moldova · by the Dniester river

By the banks of the Dniester

In Moldova, seminars are held out in the countryside near the Dniester river, where wide meadows and willow-lined banks open onto sweeping views across the valley.

It is a natural setting for outdoor circle work, Holotropic Breathwork and other practices held close to the land — a gathering place for participants from the region to deepen their practice without a long journey.
