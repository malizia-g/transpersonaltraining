Training Overview

A progressive journey from self-development through professional facilitation to advanced transpersonal psychotherapy and Holotropic Breathwork practice.

Home Training Training Overview

Training Philosophy

We recognize that healing and consciousness development require integration of psychotherapeutic expertise with understanding of transpersonal dimensions of human experience. Our training prepares practitioners who are capable of working with clients across the full spectrum of psychological and spiritual needs; from symptom relief to consciousness expansion and authentic self-realization. This requires synchronized development of rigorous psychotherapeutic skills and deep personal understanding of different states of consciousness.

Your Commitment Structure

Our training asks for consistent engagement in learning, self-development, and supervised practice. The structure below preserves the full level-based pathway so expectations are clear from the beginning.

Learning

  • Weekly Online Lessons
    • 2 hours each week
    • 40 lessons per year (80 hours)
  • Bi-Weekly Peer Group Meetings
    • Group discussion and homework integration
    • From Level 1: proposed practice for better outcomes
    • From Level 2: 20 meetings, usually around 2 hours each
  • Interview Between Levels
    • Psychological and knowledge interview
    • Between Level 1 and Level 2

Self-Development

  • Therapy with a Transpersonal Therapist
    • Level 1: 10h
    • Level 2: 20h
    • Level 3: 20h
  • Residential Trainings
    • 4 weekends per year (Thursday to Sunday)
    • 30 hours each = 120 hours/year = 480 hours in 4 years
  • 150 hours of personal development (conferences, summer school, retreats)
  • 40 sessions of Holotropic Breathwork practice

Supervision

School Track

  • Complete 50 hours of supervision for personal transpersonal psychotherapy practice with clients
    • 20 from Level 2
    • 30 from Level 3
  • This can also be done with Holotropic Breathwork floating
  • 50 hours of sessions with model clients

EUROTAS Track

  • Complete 150 hours of supervision for personal transpersonal psychotherapy practice with clients
    • 50 from Level 2
    • 100 from Level 3 (or after Level 3)
  • Clients can be people from Level 1 or model clients
  • + 300 hours of clinical practice/internships (individual and group practice)

Duration by Level

  • 1st Level: 1 year - Self-Development
  • 2nd Level: 1.5 years - Counselling Skills
  • 3rd Level: 1.5 years - Psychotherapy Skills

Program Overview

Our postgraduate program in transpersonal psychotherapy training represents a holistic approach to developing professional competence in transpersonal psychotherapy and Holotropic Breathwork. This program is designed not only for mental health professionals, educators, and practitioners but as well as, individuals motivated for self-development who are seeking to deepen their theoretical understanding and psychotherapeutic application of transpersonal psychology; a field that recognizes the spiritual dimension as integral to healing, psychological well-being and self-development.

Transpersonal psychotherapy training integrates contemporary research in psychology with established wisdom traditions, creating a comprehensive framework for understanding human consciousness, transformation, and healing. Our approach to holistic therapy acknowledges that self-development is not relying on symptomatic relief alone, but rather a process of psychological maturation and spiritual awakening that supports individuals steering through their hero journey; the archetypal process of personal transformation and growth.

Eligibility

General Eligibility (All Levels)

Applicants should demonstrate:

  • • Master's Degree in relevant field (Required for those aiming to get certified)
  • • Emotional stability and psychological readiness to engage in intensive experiential and reflective learning
  • • Capacity for self-reflection, personal responsibility, and relational maturity
  • • Openness to working with altered states of consciousness within a structured, ethical, and supervised context
  • • Willingness to engage in personal therapy and supervision as part of professional formation

Note: This training is experiential and may involve deep emotional and psychological processes. Applicants with acute, unmanaged psychiatric conditions that could be destabilized by non-ordinary states of consciousness may be advised to defer participation until clinically appropriate.

Admissions

Level 1 — Open Admission

Who Can Apply: Anyone interested in transpersonal psychology, consciousness exploration, and personal development. No formal prerequisites required.

Requirements

  1. Completed application form with personal statement
  2. Brief CV or professional background
  3. Psychological Biography
  4. Phone/video interview with program coordinator
  5. Demonstrated interest in consciousness and personal growth
Level 2 — Counseling Track

Requirements

  1. Level 1 completion documentation
  2. Personal statement describing professional goals
  3. Professional references (minimum 2)
  4. Interview with program coordinator
  5. Commitment to personal ongoing development and supervision
Level 3 — Psychotherapy Track

Requirements

  1. Level 1 and 2 completions
  2. Minimum 1-year clinical professional experience
  3. Documented transpersonal or consciousness-based work experience
  4. Clinical supervisor recommendation
  5. Detailed professional statement and clinical goals
  6. Comprehensive interview with faculty panel
  7. Commitment to ongoing personal therapy and supervision
