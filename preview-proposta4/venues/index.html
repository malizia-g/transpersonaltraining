Where we gather

Our Venues

Two inspiring settings for our residential intensives — the Black Forest and the hills of Tuscany

Home Training Venues

Our in-person intensives take place at two very different but equally inspiring locations. Each offers quiet, natural surroundings, shared living spaces and room for deep experiential work, rest and community.

Todtmoos · Black Forest, Germany

A house in the Black Forest

Our German intensives are held in a welcoming house nestled in the forested hills of Todtmoos, in the southern Black Forest. Surrounded by woodland and birdsong, it is a place designed to support deep work, rest and connection.

A light-filled training hall, shared living and dining spaces, a sunlit terrace and a garden give the group everything it needs to settle in and turn inward for the duration of the intensive.

Tenuta di Monteverdi · Tuscany, Italy

A Tuscan estate in the Maremma

Our Italian intensives take place at the Tenuta di Monteverdi, a historic estate of over 1,000 hectares in the Maremma, in southern Tuscany. It is centred on a restored 15th-century hamlet with an 18th-century church, wrapped in olive groves, vineyards, woodland and rolling hills.

Renovated farmhouses, elegant common rooms, gardens and a pool provide a serene and beautiful setting for residential intensives — with Siena, Montalcino and Florence within easy reach for those who wish to explore.
