Welcome to our school

A profession of the mind, learned with the whole heart

If you want to become a transpersonal psychotherapist* and your purpose in life is to support and help other human beings, starting with yourself, this programme is for you. Our online Transpersonal Psychotherapy Training gives you the tools and skills to work in this pioneering field, integrating transpersonal counselling with Holotropic Breathwork and somatic practice over a four-year course.

The training is a creative, innovative approach to psychotherapy and counselling, integrating developmental, relational and archetypal perspectives over a four year course.

Our team of teachers have extensive experience in the fields of Transpersonal Psychotherapy and Holotropic Breathwork. The program is based on criteria recognized by the Global Transpersonal Network EUROTAS.

Online lectures and intensive seminars in Europe · 2027–2030

Not ready for the full path? You can also join Level 1 on its own, as a standalone year of self-development, with no obligation to continue.

An honest question

Is this programme for you?

This training is for you if:

  • You feel called to support others — and to do your own deep inner work first.
  • You want to study transpersonal psychology and learn counselling skills, both online and in person.
  • You’re drawn to a therapy rooted in the body and experience, not only the intellect.
  • You’re curious about consciousness, altered states, and what lies beneath our everyday patterns.
  • You want a recognised, EUROTAS-accredited qualification in transpersonal psychotherapy.
  • You’re ready to commit to four years of study, in-person intensives, and real practice in breathwork and counselling.
