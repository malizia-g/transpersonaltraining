Our network

Collaborations

The institutes, schools and friends we walk with — past, present and future

Home Resources Collaborations

Transpersonal work has always grown through relationship. Our school is part of a living web of institutes, training schools and teachers across Europe and beyond — colleagues who share standards, students and a common vision of psychotherapy as a path of consciousness. Here we honour that network: the partners we work with today, those we have collaborated with over the years, and the connections still to come.

Present

Current partners & network

The institutes and networks we actively collaborate with today.

EUROTAS — European Transpersonal Association

Accreditation network · Europe

Our training is accredited within EUROTAS, the European Transpersonal Association, where we hold Organizational TREE membership. Belonging to this network connects our students to a Europe-wide community of transpersonal schools, shared standards and recognised certification pathways.
