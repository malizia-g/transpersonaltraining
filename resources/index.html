Recommended Reading & Watching

Books, voices and talks that have shaped our path — an invitation to explore further

Home Resources Recommended Reading

Transpersonal psychology is a wide landscape — part psychology, part philosophy, part contemplative practice. Below is a starting library of the books and thinkers we return to again and again, grouped by theme, together with a few talks worth your time. It is a living selection, refreshed as our own reading does.

Recommended reading

A themed library for the transpersonal path.

Foundations of Transpersonal Psychology

Paths Beyond Ego: The Transpersonal Vision

Roger Walsh & Frances Vaughan (eds.)

A broad, accessible map of the whole field — a good first book.

The Farther Reaches of Human Nature

Abraham Maslow

Where humanistic psychology opens into the transpersonal.

Psychosynthesis

Roberto Assagioli

Holotropic States & Breathwork

The Holotropic Mind

Stanislav Grof

Psychology of the Future

Stanislav Grof

Grof’s overview of his life’s work and the cartography of the psyche.

Stanislav & Christina Grof

Memories, Dreams, Reflections

C. G. Jung

Talks & documentaries

Omega Institute
