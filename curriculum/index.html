I

L1 - SELF DEVELOPMENT

4 modules

What you earn

Certificate of Attendance

Level 1 can be taken on its own as a standalone year of self-development — no obligation to continue.

Lessons

Module 1 - Foundations of Transpersonal Psych, History & Consciousness

This module establishes the intellectual and philosophical ground of the entire training. Students are introduced to Transpersonal Psychology as a living field, one that takes seriously the spiritual dimensions of human experience while engaging critically with its own history and limits.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

- History and core concepts of Transpersonal Psychology

- Major schools of thought and internal debates within the field

- The contributions of Stanislav Grof: his clinical-phenomenological model, its scope, and its critiques

- Positioning Transpersonal Psychology within contemporary mental health systems

- Models of Self and identity: psychological, philosophical, and contemplative perspectives

- Philosophy of life: meaning, values, and purpose as psychological processes — not abstract philosophy but lived questions

- Human needs theories: Maslow, well being communication, and contemporary perspectives

Module 2 - Consciousness, Self & the Transpersonal Lens

Before students can work with others in expanded or non-ordinary states, they need a clear and honest map of consciousness itself. This module explores what we actually mean by awareness, selfhood, and the unconscious

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

- The observer and the experiencing Self: psychological and contemplative perspectives

- Layers of the psyche: the personal and transpersonal unconscious as working models

- The “Default Mode Network” and self-referential processing: what current evidence shows and where its limits are

- States of consciousness: waking, dreaming, deep sleep, non-ordinary states, and the continuum between them

- The body-mind relationship: interoception (perception of the inner wolrd), embodied cognition, and how soma and psyche - co-arise

- Meditation and types …etc, mindfulness.

- Self-regulation as both attentional and physiological practice

- Introduction to breath as a gateway: Pranayama and shamanic breathwork understood through both physiology and attention

- Shamanic Traditions

Module 3 - Non-Ordinary States & Holotropic Breathwork (Foundations)

This module introduces the landscape of non-ordinary states — what they are, how they arise, and why they matter therapeutically. The Holotropic framework is presented as a rich phenomenological map for navigating inner experience, not as a literal account of reality. Students also receive their first honest overview of psychedelics as part of the broader NOS spectrum.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

- The spectrum of non-ordinary states: spontaneous, induced, and contemplative

- The Holotropic framework as a phenomenological map, not a literal ontology — held with curiosity and critical openness

- COEX systems, perinatal matrices, and transpersonal realms: clinical and experiential significance

- The physiology of Holotropic Breathwork: what hyperventilation actually does in the body and why this matters for safety

- Birth Turama

- Set and setting: the psychological and environmental variables that shape any non-ordinary state experience

- Contraindications and the ethical safety framework for breathwork

- Breather and sitter roles: responsibilities, relational quality, and the ethics of presence

- Psychedelics as a category of non-ordinary states: historical context, key substances (psilocybin, MDMA, LSD, ayahuasca), phenomenology, risks, and legal context

- Comparing NOS pathways: breathwork, meditation, psychedelics, dreaming, and spontaneous mystical experience

- Therapeutic potential and risks across modalities — a critical and grounded overview

Module 4 - Spiritual Development, Crisis & Integration

Models of spiritual development across cultures. Spiritual emergence vs. psychological crisis. Phenomenology of mystical experiences and dark night processes. Foundations of integration: grounding, meaning-making, embodiment, and psychological containment.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

- Models of spiritual development across traditions: comparative and non-dogmatic

- Spiritual emergence vs. psychological crisis: how to recognise and differentiate

- Phenomenology of mystical experiences, “Dark night processes”, and “Ego Death”

- Integration as a psychological process: grounding, meaning-making, embodiment, and time

- Shadow work understood through the lens of defence mechanisms and unconscious processes

- Inner child work and transgenerational influences: Gestalt and systemic perspectives

- Developing a personal spiritual practice: journaling, meditation, and reflective documentation

- Personal spirituality

- Spiritual bypass

L2 - COUNSELLING SKILLS & PROFESSIONAL FACILITATION

6 modules

What you earn

Certificate in Transpersonal Counselling Skills

Awarded through a EUROTAS-accredited institute, with credits toward EUROTAS certification.

Lessons

Module 5 - Clinical Foundations: Theories of Psychotherapy & Transpersonal Integration

Transpersonal practitioners need fluency in the established landscape of psychotherapy — not to choose a single school, but to think clearly across models and locate their work within the broader field. This module builds that literacy, with critical engagement rather than passive survey.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

- Overview of major psychotherapy models: psychodynamic, attachment-based, humanistic-existential, somatic, and trauma-informed

- How different models conceptualise suffering, the psyche, and the mechanism of change

- Strengths and limitations of each approach — engaged criticallyIntegrating transpersonal perspectives within established therapeutic frameworks

- The distinction between counselling and psychotherapy: depth, duration, focus, and professional scope

Module 6 - Counselling Skills, Therapeutic Relationship & Presence

Core counselling skills: empathic listening, attunement, reflective responding, emotion-focused work, establishing therapeutic alliance, boundaries, and basic assessment. Working with relational dynamics and ethical presence in one-to-one and group contexts.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

Module 7 - Facilitation of Non-Ordinary States & Group Process

Professional facilitation of breathwork and consciousness practices: session design, music sequencing, non-directive, bodywork principles, containment of emotional material, group dynamics, and post-session integration practices.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

Module 8 - Professional Ethics, Boundaries & Continuing Development

Deepening ethical practice for professional transpersonal work. Maintaining clear professional boundaries while facilitating deep consciousness work. Learning when and how to seek supervision and consultation. Establishing Ethical, sustainable professional practices with emphasis on the concept of consent and bodywork.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

Module 9 - Somatic Awareness, Trauma-Informed Practice & Integration

Nervous system regulation, trauma-aware facilitation, grounding and stabilization skills, working with dissociation and overwhelm, and integration practices that support psychological safety and continuity of change.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

Module 10 - Advanced Psychotherapeutic Skills & Clinical Process

Advanced therapeutic skills: deep listening, moment-to-moment process tracking, rupture and repair, transference/countertransference, pacing depth, working with resistance, and maintaining therapeutic alliance in complex clinical work.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

III

L3 - PSYCHOTHERAPY & HOLOTROPIC BREATHWORK PRACTITIONER

5 modules

What you earn

Certificate as Transpersonal Psychotherapy & Holotropic Breathwork Facilitator

With an optional EUROTAS certification track. Please note: “psychotherapist” is a legally protected title in some countries (for example Italy). We are working toward EAP compliance, but at present no transpersonal school is EAP-accredited — please check the psychotherapy regulations in your own country before using the title professionally.

Lessons

Module 11 - Transpersonal Interventions & Tools in Psychotherapy

Clinical use of transpersonal tools: imaginal work, symbolic dialogue, archetypal processes, ritual elements, therapeutic use of spiritual frameworks, dreamwork, and meaning-oriented interventions. Indications, contraindications, and ethical application in psychotherapy.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

Module 12 - Trauma-Informed Transpersonal Psychotherapy

Advanced work with developmental trauma, attachment injury, dissociation, and shame-based organization. Integrating somatic regulation, parts-based models, and consciousness-oriented interventions within phase-oriented trauma treatment.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

Module 13 - Clinical Assessment, Differential Diagnosis & Treatment Planning

Advanced assessment integrating diagnosis manuals - informed diagnosis with transpersonal perspectives. Differential diagnosis between spiritual emergence, trauma responses, dissociation, and psychiatric pathology. Phase-oriented treatment planning, risk management, and collaboration with mental health professionals

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)

Module 14 - Consciousness-Based Psychotherapy & Holotropic Breathwork (Advanced Integration)

Clinical integration of Holotropic Breathwork and non-ordinary state work within longer-term psychotherapy. Indications, contraindications, clinical timing, integration planning, working with grief, existential crisis, identity transformation, and sustaining therapeutic outcomes beyond peak experiences.

20 Weekly Online (2 hrs)
