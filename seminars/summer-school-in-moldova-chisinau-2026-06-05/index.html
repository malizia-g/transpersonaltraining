Summer School in Moldova

5–7 June 2026 · Moldova

Home Seminars Summer School in Moldova (2026)

This seminar took place in 2026. See the upcoming dates.

A multi-day summer school combining transpersonal theory with a closing group ceremony. The ceremony uses ritual and collective presence to help integrate what has emerged during the training and restore a felt sense of belonging.

Optional seminars are open to students as additional practice and self-exploration; they count towards the personal experience in expanded states that the training requires, but are not part of the compulsory modules.

Getting there

This seminar is held in Chisinau, Moldova. Directions are sent to participants with their booking confirmation.
