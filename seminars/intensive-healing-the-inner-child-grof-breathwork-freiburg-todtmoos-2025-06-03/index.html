Holotropic Breathwork seminar · Freiburg

Intensive: Healing the Inner Child + Grof® Breathwork

3–6 June 2025 · Haus am Mühlenbach, Todtmoos

Home Seminars Intensive: Healing the Inner Child + Grof® Breathwork (2025)

This seminar took place in 2025. See the upcoming dates.

Rainer Pervoltz combines inner-child healing with a Holotropic Breathwork session, working from the principle that the therapist's role is guidance, not solutions. Bodywork and breathwork open access to early wounds, met with presence, acceptance and non-judgment.

This residential intensive is part of Module 6 of the four-year Transpersonal Psychotherapy & Holotropic Breathwork training.

Getting there

A seminar house in the southern Black Forest, about an hour from Freiburg, Basel and Zurich.

Train & bus

Travel to Basel Badischer Bahnhof (Basel Bad Bf) and change to the local train towards Waldshut. Get off at Bad Säckingen and take bus 7320 to Todtmoos-Au, where the seminar house is. Current connections: int.bahn.de.

By car

Take the A5 towards Basel as far as the Weil am Rhein junction, then the A98 to Lörrach and the B317 to Schopfheim. From there the B518 to Wehr, and from Wehr the L148 to Todtmoos. The first village after the Wehratal gorge is Todtmoos-Au: take the first road on the right up the hill, then the next left. The seminar house is the last house on the right.

By plane

Basel/Mulhouse and Zurich airports are about an hour’s drive away. Public transport from Basel airport works but is complicated — leave the airport on the Swiss side and search “Basel Flughafen” to “Todtmoos Au” on int.bahn.de. Buses from Basel airport to Freiburg (FlixBus) leave from the French side.

  • Mobile phone reception is usually only possible outside the house; Wi-Fi is available.
  • Before each intensive the participant mailing list is opened to everyone, so you can arrange to drive together.
