Home Seminars

The residential part of the training: intensives that pair a seminar topic with Holotropic Breathwork sessions, and optional breathwork weekends for extra practice. Most take place at the Haus am Mühlenbach in Todtmoos, in the Black Forest near Freiburg; others in Tuscany, Moldova, Latvia, Romania and Italy. For filters and the current season see the intensive schedule.
