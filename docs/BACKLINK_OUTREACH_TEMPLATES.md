# Backlink Outreach — Ready-to-Send Templates

> Companion to **[MARKETING_AND_SEO.md](MARKETING_AND_SEO.md) → Phase H / "Backlinks & Partner
> Outreach"**. Use these two templates to ask partner schools, teachers and friends of the
> school to add a link to us from their website — a reciprocal link exchange. Address each
> person **by first name**. Send the email to institute directors / teachers you know well;
> use the WhatsApp version for quick, informal contacts.
>
> **The ask, in one line:** their site adds a plain, *followed* link to
> `https://transpersonal-training.com` (natural anchor text such as *"Eastwest Transpersonal
> Training School"* or *"I teach at Eastwest Transpersonal Training School"*); in return we add
> a link to their site on our **[Collaborations page](https://transpersonal-training.com/collaborations/)**.
>
> Track who has been asked / who links back in the worksheet tables in MARKETING_AND_SEO.md.

*Created: July 2026*

---

## What to tell their webmaster (paste-in checklist)

When someone says "just tell me what my web person needs", send them this:

- **Anchor text (the clickable words):** e.g. *Eastwest Transpersonal Training School*
- **Link destination (URL):** `https://transpersonal-training.com`
- **Link type:** a normal, *followed* link — please **not** `rel="nofollow"`
- **Where:** a partners / links / "schools we work with" page, or an "I teach at…" line on a bio

## Email template

**Subject:** A small favour — linking our schools together

> Hi **[First name]**,
>
> I hope this finds you well.
>
> We've just rebuilt the **Eastwest Transpersonal Training School** website
> (transpersonal-training.com), and as part of the relaunch we're strengthening the web of
> connections between the schools, teachers and friends who share our transpersonal path.
>
> Would you be willing to add a link to our school from your website? A simple, ordinary link —
> something like *"In partnership with Eastwest Transpersonal Training School"* or *"I teach
> at…"* pointing to https://transpersonal-training.com. It helps people find us and, just as
> importantly, it tells search engines that our schools stand together — one of the most
> valuable things colleagues in our field can do for one another.
>
> Of course, we'd be delighted to do the same for you. We've created a **Collaborations page**
> where we celebrate the institutes, teachers and friends we walk with — past, present and
> future. Send me your website address and the wording you'd like, and I'll add a link back to
> you there right away: https://transpersonal-training.com/collaborations/
>
> If it's easiest, just forward this to whoever looks after your website. All they need is:
>
> - **The link text:** e.g. "Eastwest Transpersonal Training School"
> - **Where it points:** https://transpersonal-training.com
> - Ideally a normal, *followed* link (not "nofollow")
>
> Thank you, **[First name]** — it means a lot to keep our community connected.
>
> Warmly,
> **[Your name]**
> Eastwest Transpersonal Training School
> transpersonal-training.com
