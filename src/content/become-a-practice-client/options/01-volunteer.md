During online lessons and intensives, a teacher — or a student, with the teacher present — works with a real person to demonstrate a technique. You can volunteer to be that person. It is a genuine session — but it takes place **in front of the training group**, who observe and learn from it.

- Led by a teacher, or by a student with the teacher present
- The student group watches and takes part in the discussion
- Online or during a residential intensive
- Volunteers are selected through an interview
- No fee · limited places, on request
