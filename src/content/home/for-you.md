---
heading: "Is this programme for you?"
intro: "This training is for you if:"
---

- You feel called to support others — and to do your own deep inner work first.
- You want to study transpersonal psychology and learn counselling skills, both online and in person.
- You're drawn to a therapy rooted in the body and experience, not only the intellect.
- You're curious about consciousness, altered states, and what lies beneath our everyday patterns.
- You want a recognised, EUROTAS-accredited qualification in transpersonal psychotherapy.
- You're ready to commit to four years of study, in-person intensives, and real practice in breathwork and counselling.
