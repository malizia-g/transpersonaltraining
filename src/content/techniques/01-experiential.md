### Holotropic Breathwork
![Mandala drawing during a Holotropic Breathwork session](/assets/images/Techniques/technique-mandala-breathwork "object-[50%_70%]")

The heart of the experiential training. Developed by Stanislav Grof, it combines accelerated breathing, evocative music and focused bodywork to enter non-ordinary states of consciousness. Breath acts as a "controllable stress" that gives access to biographical, perinatal and transpersonal material; sessions are integrated through mandala drawing, sharing and bodywork.

In the school's language, the continuous breath puts you in contact with your boundaries — and boundaries are doors.

### Working with Groups
![Hands gathered around a candle at the centre of a mandala](/assets/images/Techniques/technique-mandala-drawing "object-center")

The residential intensives and weekly meetings are where theory becomes lived experience. In the group, students apply the frameworks studied in the lectures, practice one-to-one sessions with each other under supervision, and go through somatic and body-oriented work — movement, contact, trust and sensory exercises — alongside meditation and shared silence. The group itself becomes a tuned field: a container of co-regulation where each nervous system supports the others, and where the material that a single person cannot hold alone can finally move.

### Trance, Ritual & Expressive Tools
![A drawing from a session: the languages of image and symbol](/assets/images/Symbolic/symbolic-mandala-collection "object-[50%_82%]")

Across the training, trance is treated as the working medium of transpersonal therapy: drumming and rhythm, guided imagery, dream-body movement, metaphoric cards, mandalas and drawing all serve to bypass the rational "critical factors" and let the deeper languages of consciousness — sensation, emotion, image, symbol — speak.
