heading: Program Overview
---

Our postgraduate program in transpersonal psychotherapy training represents a holistic approach to developing professional competence in transpersonal psychotherapy and Holotropic Breathwork. This program is designed not only for mental health professionals, educators, and practitioners, but also for those interested in self-development, seeking to deepen their understanding of transpersonal psychology.

Transpersonal psychotherapy training integrates contemporary research in psychology with wisdom traditions, creating a comprehensive framework for understanding human consciousness, transformation, and healing. Our approach to holistic therapy acknowledges that self-development is not exclusively about symptomatic relief. Rather, it is about truly understanding what it means to be human, fully integrating all our humanity, a process of maturation from the wounded child so we can live from a space of freedom.
