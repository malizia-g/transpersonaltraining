intro: This training may be for you if you are asking deeper questions about yourself, consciousness and what it means to be human.
closing: Are you looking for an accredited online training in transpersonal psychotherapy that combines rigorous study with experiential and embodied learning?
---

- Are you interested in a pioneering approach to psychotherapy?
- Are you curious about the nature of consciousness and the possibilities of human experience?
- Do you want to explore the philosophy of ontology and what it means to be a human being? Why are we here? What is our purpose?
- Are you ready to embark on your own Hero's Journey of exploration and transformation?
- Do you want to understand who you are beyond the patterns, habits and conditioning that unconsciously shape your life?
- Do you want to know yourself more deeply, in all your complexity and humanity?
- Are you interested in exploring altered states of consciousness and their transformative potential?

### Bringing This into Your Life and Work

- Are you keen to weave transpersonal frameworks and perspectives into your life and professional practice?
- Do you want to explore how transpersonal theories and practices can create powerful transformative experiences for both you and your clients?
- Are you ready for the commitment?
- Are you willing to commit to four years of study, including in-person intensives and substantial hours of practice in breathwork and counselling?
