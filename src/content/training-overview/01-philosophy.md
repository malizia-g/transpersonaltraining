---
heading: Cultivating an Inner Space
---

This is more than just a professional training. It is for people whose purpose is to work with, support, and empower human beings.

It is an invitation to become a space for deeply profound transformation — beginning with yourself — through the pioneering approach of Transpersonal Psychotherapy.

The course follows an integrative approach, teaching therapeutic techniques, diagnostic and theoretical models, embodiment practices and healing nutrition. These constitute the building blocks of our training. In parallel to these, students will be cultivating an inner space to come from when working with clients.

EUROTAS-accredited, this transpersonal psychology degree is recognized across Europe and structured to meet international standards for transpersonal practice.
