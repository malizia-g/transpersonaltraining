**School Track:**

- Complete 50 hours of supervision for personal transpersonal psychotherapy practice with clients
  - 20 from Level 2
  - 30 from Level 3
- This can also be done with Holotropic Breathwork floating
- 50 hours of sessions with model clients

**EUROTAS Track:**

- Complete 150 hours of supervision for personal transpersonal psychotherapy practice with clients
  - 50 from Level 2
  - 100 from Level 3 (or after Level 3)
- Clients can be people from Level 1 or model clients
- \+ 300 hours of clinical practice/internships (individual and group practice)
