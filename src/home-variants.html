Internal — not linked from the site

Six homepages, two questions:
how warm, and which footage?

Every version keeps the same structure, the same words and scientific blue as the leading colour. They differ in warmth — how much gold, cream and terracotta sits alongside the blue, and how many human, lamplit photographs appear — and, for the two warmest, in the film behind the opening screen.

All six also fix the hero video: the old page laid an opaque white gradient over its lower half, which is what read as a “white filter”. That is gone everywhere.

The mountain stream, as today

A

Lightly warmed

Today’s page with the washed-out video fixed and nothing else changed. The control.

B

Gently warm

Warmer paper throughout, stronger gold rules, a touch of warmth graded into the video, two lamplit photographs in the film strip.

The three new films

The C pair differs only in the footage — autumn leaves against the aerial sunset — so any difference you feel there is the film alone. The D pair changes two things at once and is not a clean comparison: D1 carries the “less crowded” edit as well as the leaves, while D2 keeps the full page behind the dawn clip.

C1

Warm · autumn leaves

Terracotta joins the gold, cards sit on cream, the contact band moves to the fire pit. Backlit beech leaves behind the opening screen.

C2

Warm · forest sunset

The same treatment as C1, with the sun setting over woodland seen from the air.

D1

Strongly warm · autumn leaves · less crowded

A full cream palette, terracotta headings, a golden-hour glow in the hero and the serif voice carried into it. Beech leaves behind.

Also trims the page: the closing welcome paragraph goes, the three “roots” cards become a single link to Techniques, and the six-point list folds away behind its heading.

D2

Strongly warm · misty valley at dawn

The same warmth as D1 but with the page untrimmed, behind the third clip: sunrise over wooded hills with mist in the valleys.

The autumn-leaves clip is still the free iStock preview, so it carries a watermark across the middle and tops out at 768px wide. Judge the mood, not the sharpness — the purchased file replaces it before anything goes live.
