Internal — not linked from the site

Four homepages, one question:
which one feels warm enough?

All four keep the same structure, the same words and scientific blue as the leading colour. They differ only in warmth — how much gold, cream and terracotta sits alongside the blue, how warm the hero footage is graded, and how many human, lamplit photographs appear.

Every version also fixes the hero video: the old page laid an opaque white gradient over its lower half, which is what read as a “white filter”. That is gone in all four.

A

Lightly warmed

Today’s page with the washed-out video fixed and nothing else changed. The control.

B

Gently warm

Warmer paper throughout, stronger gold rules, a touch of warmth graded into the video, two lamplit photographs in the film strip.

C

Warm

Terracotta joins the gold, cards sit on cream, the contact band moves to the fire pit, and new photographs from the training hall appear.

D

Strongly warm

A full cream palette, terracotta headings, brighter gold, the quote band drifting towards plum, and the serif voice carried into the hero.

The navigation on each variant still points at the live site, so use the browser back button to return here. These pages carry noindex and stay out of the sitemap.
