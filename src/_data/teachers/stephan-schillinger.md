## Biography

Stephan Schillinger was a competitive athlete at world-championship level until age 21, before a twelve-year career in finance as Vice-President and Relationship Manager at Crédit Suisse Basel and Director at Richelieu Banque Privée Strasbourg, supporting business leaders and celebrities with wealth management and life transitions. Beginning in 2017, he underwent a significant personal transformation, pursuing spiritual development through shamanism, meditation, fasting and plant medicine across Peru, Switzerland, Belgium, Spain, Thailand and Canada — including three months in the Amazon with the Shipibo community in 2020. In 2018 he founded "Par un Curieux Hasard", a personal-development platform that reached 8 million views and 36,000 subscribers within two years and led to several published books. He now offers individual consultations, workshops and retreats focused on trauma healing and inner transformation.
