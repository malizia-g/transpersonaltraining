## Biography

Lev Belogorodskii is a psychotherapist trained in multiple modalities including Vivation, Biosynthesis, Biodynamic therapy, Family Constellations and Process Work under instructors such as David Boadella and Arnold Mindell. He has worked with individuals and groups in psychology, psychotherapy, coaching and business consulting for over 27 years.

## Credentials

- Registered psychotherapist, European Association for Psychotherapy (EAP) and World Council for Psychotherapy (WCPC)
- Full member, European Association for Body Psychotherapy (EABP)
- Co-founder of Resource-Oriented System Therapy (ROST) with M. Sandomirskiy
- Moscow Institute of Aviation Engineers; Moscow State University, Faculty of Psychology
