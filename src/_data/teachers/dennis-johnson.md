## Biography

Dennis Johnson maintains an academic foundation in Tibetan and Buddhist Studies and has served as a librarian, translator, editor and interpreter. His professional experience includes roles at Windhorse Integral Mental Health Vienna and the Oxford Mindfulness Centre. His scholarly focus centers on transdisciplinary and transcultural research and practice, with applications toward individual, social and cultural transformation grounded in both traditional knowledge and modern science.

## Credentials

- Master's degree in Tibetan and Buddhist Studies, Vienna University
- Propaedeutic Psychotherapy Training, Vienna University Postgraduate Center
- Advanced Training in Transpersonal Psychology and Holotropic Breathwork
- Training in Contemplative Psychology and Psychosocial Interventions
