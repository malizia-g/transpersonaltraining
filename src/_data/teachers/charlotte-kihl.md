## Biography

Charlotte Kihl transitioned mid-career to pursue psychosynthesis training, which has remained her foundational practice for over 20 years. She supervises fourth-year psychosynthesis students in Sweden and previously taught at coaching institutes. Her expertise spans individual and couples therapy, family work, supervision, mindfulness instruction and transpersonal programs through her Stockholm practice, Grow Beyond.

## Credentials

- Certified Psychosynthesis Therapist (PsA®) and Supervisor (PsA®)
- Certified Professional Coach (ICF)
- Certified Mindfulness Instructor
- Certificate in Biotransenergetics BODY MIND Work
- Training in Imago Couples Therapy
- Studies at Stockholm University, INSEAD and London Business School
