## Biography

Ingrida Indane founded and directs the Transpersonal Education Institute in Latvia. She serves as executive director of the European Transpersonal Association and coordinates its Certification and Accreditation Committee. Her career spans art therapy, transpersonal psychology education and extensive international conference presentations, having conducted trainings in transpersonal arts therapy and psychology across Latvia and internationally since 1995.

## Credentials

- PhD studies, Ubiquity University's Integral Transpersonal Psychology program (2020–present)
- Master's degree in Arts, Latvian Academy of Arts (2000)
- Bachelor of Social Science in Psychology, International Institute of Practical Psychology (1996–2001)
- Bachelor of Fine Arts, Latvian Academy of Arts (1986–1992)
- Supervisor training by C. Sibbett and W. Thompson, Riga Stradiņš University (2007)
