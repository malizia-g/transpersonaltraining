## Biography

Rainer Pervöltz studied German and French language and literature before relocating to California, where he trained in Gestalt and body psychotherapy with Jack Lee Rosenberg and Eric Marcus in Los Angeles, and later with Gerda Boyesen in London. He served as a trainer for the Biodynamic Institute during the 1980s–90s and co-founded the Chiron Centre in London in 1983. His spiritual education included work with Andrew DaPassano, Mantak Chia (Taoist Energy Training), Arnold Mindell (Dream Body) and Jaya Herbst (Psychic Healing). He now practices as a therapist, supervisor and teacher across Europe and co-directs the International Institute for Consciousness Exploration and Psychotherapy in Freiburg (IIBP).
