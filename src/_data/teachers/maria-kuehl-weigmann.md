## Biography

Maria Kuehl-Weigmann brought decades of clinical experience to the school through long-term work in student counseling at the Ruhr-University in Bochum and in her own practice, with specialized training across multiple therapeutic modalities. She spent four years working at Findhorn and remained closely involved with transpersonal training programs throughout her career. She passed away in 2023, remembered fondly as part of the school's teaching history.

## Credentials

- Professional Association of German Psychotherapists (Clinical Psychotherapist)
- Extensive training in Gestalt therapy, Process-Oriented Psychotherapy, Karma and Biography-Work, Prayer of the Heart, and Trauma Therapy
- 25-year student of the Ridhwan School and Diamond Approach with A.H. Almaas
