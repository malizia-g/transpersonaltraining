## Biography

Manal Al-Hammadi is a certified Psychedelic Integration Facilitator and Educator and Managing Director of the Psychedelic Studies Institute in Poland. Originally from Yemen and now based in Poland, she brings together expertise in innovation, psychedelic research and education, with over a decade of experience studying psychedelics. She has a career in technology and innovation and leads initiatives advancing training on therapeutic psychedelic use within legal and ethical frameworks, emphasizing the bridging of ancestral knowledge with emerging research for holistic, interconnected healing.

## Credentials

- Certified Psychedelic Integration Facilitator and Educator (USA)
- Currently training as a transpersonal psychotherapist
- Pursuing a Ph.D. at the intersection of psychedelics, public health, and drug policy

## Experience

Managing Director, Psychedelic Studies Institute (Poland); over a decade of experience in psychedelic research, education and integration.
