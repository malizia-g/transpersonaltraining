## Biography

Samvedam B. Randles directs the Inner Arts Institute in Watertown, Massachusetts, where she trains and teaches Breathwork, Systemic Constellation Work, and Trauma and Collective Trauma Integration Work. She has 20 years of breathwork instruction experience, founded the Center for Body-oriented Psychotherapy in Boston, and brings 18 years of constellation facilitation experience plus 15 years training facilitators in the modality.

## Credentials

- Licensed Mental Health Counselor (Massachusetts)
- Certified in Postural Integration by Jack Painter, PhD
- Certified in Breathwork by the International Breathwork Foundation
- Trained in Pulsation (Reichian Breathwork) at Shree Rajneesh Ashram, India
- Trained in Systemic Constellation Work by Bert Hellinger
- Training in Collective Trauma Integration Process with Thomas Hübl
