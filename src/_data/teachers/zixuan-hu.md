## Biography

Zixuan Hu holds a master's degree in Investment Management from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and is a doctoral researcher at the University of Northampton, England. Her PhD explores contemporary experiences and practices associated with the Akashic Records, with particular attention to altered states of consciousness, anomalous information reception, meaning-making and their perceived therapeutic potential. Her wider interests include transpersonal psychology, parapsychology, spiritual practices and non-ordinary experiences. Through her research she hopes to bring a little-studied contemporary spiritual practice into closer dialogue with academic approaches to human consciousness, healing and transformation.
