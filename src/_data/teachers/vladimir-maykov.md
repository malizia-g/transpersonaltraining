## Biography

Recognized as the pioneer of transpersonal studies in Russia, Vladimir Maykov established over 15 training programs in transpersonal psychology and psychotherapy. He led an international project that produced roughly 80 transpersonal psychology texts in Russian, founded a video archive of over 500 free transpersonal resources, and directed three documentary films.

## Credentials

- Founding President and board member, Russian Association for Transpersonal Psychology and Psychotherapy
- Senior research associate, Institute of Philosophy of the Russian Academy of Sciences
