## Biography

Lilian Gscheidel is part of the training team at Grof® Legacy Training Germany. She runs a private Gestalt therapy practice in Berlin, offers Grof® Breathwork weekends, and facilitates therapeutic aftercare groups for psychosomatic clinic patients, having lived in community for many years.

## Credentials

- Psychology degree (BSc, University of Magdeburg)
- Certified Transpersonal Breathwork Practitioner
- Certified in Grof® Legacy Training
- Certified Gestalt Therapist and Dance Therapist
- Certified Hero's Journey Facilitator; Psychedelic Facilitator certification
