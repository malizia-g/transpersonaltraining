## Biography

Mario Lorenzetti brings over two decades of facilitation experience and developed "Holo Sacred Breathwork," blending shamanic and holotropic approaches. He maintains an extensive practice studying non-ordinary states of consciousness, has served as an editor for the Italian Society of Studies on States of Consciousness, co-founded the International Ecopsychology Society, and follows a long path of Zen practice. His research spans consciousness studies and Aldous Huxley scholarship.

## Credentials

- Certified in Transpersonal Psychology and Holotropic Breathwork by Stanislav Grof
- Certified in shamanic counseling by C. Michael Smith (Crows Nest Center for Shamanic Studies)
- Training in conflict management and group dynamics (IBTG Institute)
- Advanced seminars, Foundation for Shamanic Studies
