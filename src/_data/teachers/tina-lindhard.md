## Biography

Tina Lindhard holds a doctorate in consciousness studies and specializes in transpersonal psychology with an emphasis on spirituality. She serves as a mentor for graduate students at IUPS and maintains a private practice blending meditation, craniosacral training and psychology, with a particular focus on trauma and grief work. An experienced educator, she has taught Arka Dhyana meditation internationally across Spain, Russia, France, Italy and the United States. Her scholarly work examines levels of consciousness, embryology, intuition, and the intersection of spirituality with mental health. She founded and chairs CCA Spain and sits on the board of CICA International, a consciousness research organization.

## Credentials

- PhD Consciousness Studies, International University of Professional Studies (IUPS), Hawaii (2016)
- MATP Transpersonal Psychology, Sofia University/ITP, Palo Alto, CA (2013)
- Specialized diplomas in craniosacral therapy, kinesiology, family constellation therapy and osteopathy
- Bachelor of Social Science (Honors), University of Cape Town, South Africa (1976)
