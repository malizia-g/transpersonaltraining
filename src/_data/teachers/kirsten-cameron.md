## Biography

Kirsten Cameron is a researcher, writer and educator whose work bridges transpersonal psychology, trauma studies and consciousness research. Her doctoral dissertation examined precognition as an adaptive response to childhood trauma. She has published in peer-reviewed journals including the International Journal of Transpersonal Studies and the Journal of Anomalous Experience and Cognition, and contributed to the Institute of Noetic Sciences. Her teaching emphasizes embodied awareness, intellectual curiosity, and the creative integration of science and spirit.
