## Biography

Dr. Steven Schmitz holds a PhD in Transpersonal Psychology from the Institute of Transpersonal Psychology (USA) and is a shamanic practitioner and teacher, international lecturer and workshop leader on shamanism, ritual, healing and transpersonal psychology. He has been engaged in the transpersonal field since the mid-1970s and was initiated into both shamanic practice and the practice of zazen in 1974. He is a Minister with Circle of the Sacred Earth, Inc., was initiated into the ancient Inca Mystical Path in 2009, and is a mesa carrier.

He currently teaches at the Alef Trust (UK), the Latvian Transpersonal Educational Institute (Riga) and the Athens Synthesis Center (Greece), and has taught at the Institute of Transpersonal Psychology, Sofia University and the California Institute of Integral Studies (USA), and the Integral Transpersonal Institute (Milan). Alongside his teaching he has a private practice in shamanic counseling, shamanic healing and spiritual guidance, leads monthly Shamanic Journey Circles in Latvia, Greece, Russia and the UK, and offers shamanic ceremonies.
