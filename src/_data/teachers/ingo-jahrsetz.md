## Biography

Ingo Benjamin Jahrsetz conducts seminars and trainings in transpersonal psychotherapy, spiritual guidance and Grof®Breathwork in Germany and abroad, and provides supervision in transpersonal psychotherapy. His published work includes the book "Holotropes Atmen – Psychotherapie und Spiritualität" (Verlag Klett-Cotta), along with numerous articles in professional journals.

## Credentials

- Teacher within the Grof Legacy Program
- Co-Founder and director, International Institute for Consciousness Exploration
- Founder and honorary chairman, Spiritual Emergence Network (SEN) Germany
- Former President of EUROTAS
