## Biography

Cristina Revenco practices as a licensed transpersonal psychotherapist with specialized experience working with children and adolescents facing developmental challenges, including autism spectrum disorders and ADHD. She founded Psycharmonia, a professional project, and regularly conducts webinars and workshops. Her training encompasses breathwork techniques, integral psychotherapy, art therapy, hypnosis and mindfulness practices, alongside broader interests in religious studies, mythology, philosophy and the arts.

## Credentials

- Bachelor in Psychology
- Master Degree in Clinical Psychology and Counselling
- Transpersonal Psychotherapy training through the European Transpersonal Association (ETPA)
- ETPA member; EUROTAS member
