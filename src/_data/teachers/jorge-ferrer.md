## Biography

Jorge N. Ferrer, PhD, is an internationally recognized scholar, psychologist, and author best known for developing the participatory approach to spirituality and consciousness studies. He is Associate Research Faculty at the California Institute for Human Science and served for more than two decades as a Professor of Psychology at the California Institute of Integral Studies, where he also chaired the Department of East-West Psychology. A former advisor to the United Nations-affiliated organization Religions for Peace and a member of the Esalen Institute's Center for Theory and Research, Ferrer received the Fetzer Institute's Presidential Award for his pioneering contributions to the study of human consciousness.

Award-winning author of several books, including _Revisioning Transpersonal Theory_, _Participation and the Mystery_, and _Love and Freedom_, with his forthcoming _The Participatory Way: Reimagining Consciousness, Psychedelics, Embodiment, and Culture_.
