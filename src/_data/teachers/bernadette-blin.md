## Biography

Bernadette Blin is a clinical and social psychologist trained in transpersonal psychotherapy and Gestalt therapy, and a former President of EUROTAS. She has been initiated by shamans from different traditions across Mexico, Morocco, Peru, Siberia and the United States. She founded the Evolutive Relaxation method in 1983 and trains transpersonal psychotherapists internationally, serving with CesHum (the French Transpersonal School) and IIBP (the International Institute for Consciousness Exploration and Psychotherapy) in Germany. She has authored and co-authored multiple works on transpersonal psychotherapy and emphasizes opening our heart and consciousness to create a more sustainable world.
