## Biography

Rita Aguila brings a transpersonal approach and a specialization in trauma, with over 15 years exploring psychedelics therapeutically. She maintains a private practice serving teenagers and adults, facilitates women's and couples' retreats, and collaborates with Kosmicare at major festivals including BOOM.

## Credentials

- Bachelor's degree in Clinical Psychology (transpersonal approach, trauma specialization)
- Postgraduate degree in Hypnotherapy
- Master's degree in Music Therapy
- Psychotraumatology certification
- Psychedelic-Assisted Psychotherapy degree (MAPS and California Institute of Integral Studies)
