## Biography

Gabriel Fernandez-Borsot is core faculty at the Alef Trust, teaching transpersonal psychology and psychotherapy within their accredited MSc program and coordinating certificate programs there, alongside teaching at the International University of Catalonia. He has run a private practice since 2012 specializing in transpersonal therapy, and has trained psychotherapists since 2018. His research addresses transpersonal psychotherapy, transpersonal theories, the transdisciplinary analysis of otherness, the anthropology of technology, and AI ethics. He integrates spiritual seeking with psychological frameworks and is also a published singer-songwriter.

## Credentials

- PhD in Transformative Studies, California Institute of Integral Studies (San Francisco)
- Degree in Philosophy, University of Barcelona
- Degree in Industrial Engineering, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya
- Professional member of EUROTAS
- Member, Spanish Association of Gestalt Therapy (AETG)

## Experience

Core faculty, Alef Trust; teaches at the International University of Catalonia; private transpersonal therapy practice since 2012.
