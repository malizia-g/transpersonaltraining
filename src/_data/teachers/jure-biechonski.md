## Biography

Jure Biechonski is a counselling psychologist and founder and director of SACH International and the TERVIKLIK MINA School. He brings 35 years of practice as a psychotherapist and nine years of advanced study in hypnotherapy and NLP to the course. He founded THA (Transpersonal Hypno-Analysis) and teaches at institutions in China, Estonia and Mexico while lecturing globally, using hypnotherapy to accelerate the therapeutic process by accessing the subconscious mind.

## Credentials

- Ph.D. candidate, Christ Church University
- Academic degrees in History, Philosophy, Education, French Literature and Psychology
- Graduate, The Atkinson Ball College of Hypnotherapy & Hypnohealing
- Three years of study at The U.K. College of Hypnotherapy & Counselling
- Training in Humanistic Counselling (3 years) and Family Counselling (2 years)
- Additional training in Psychodrama and Art & Dance Therapy

## Experience

35 years of practice as a psychotherapist; founder of Transpersonal Hypno-Analysis (THA); teaches in China, Estonia and Mexico.
