## Biography

Serge Beddington-Behrens is an Oxford-educated professional with forty years of experience conducting spiritual retreats worldwide. He co-founded the Institute for the Study of Conscious Evolution in San Francisco during the 1980s and authored "Awakening the Universal Heart" and "Gateways to the Soul" (2020), with "Amazing Grace" released in 2023. He divides his time between London and Mallorca.
