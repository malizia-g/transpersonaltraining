## Biography

Lyudmila Skartsesku is Head East of the Transpersonal Training program and a certified transpersonal psychotherapist. She is President of the Association of Transpersonal Development in Moldova, a Holotropic Breathwork Leader, a board member of EUROTAS and coordinator of its Ethics Committee. She specializes in intense integrative psychotherapeutic methods and teaches Process Oriented Therapy and Transpersonal Psychology. Her research interests span shamanic states of consciousness, spiritual crisis intervention, and the influence of quantum processes on consciousness. She conducts training programs across Moldova, Romania and Latvia.
