## Biography

Liudmila Serbina is a Ph.D. psychotherapist of the European Association of Psychotherapists (EAP) and Scientific Director of the Institute of Integrative Psychology and Psychotherapy training program. Practicing since 1995, she has studied Process Work directly under Arnold and Amy Mindell since 1999. She has conducted psychotherapeutic groups, authored seminars, and organized international conferences throughout Russia and the CIS.

## Credentials

- International Diploma and Certificate in Process Work (Process Work Center of Portland, USA)
- Member, IAPOP (International Association of Process Oriented Psychology)
- Head of Process-Oriented Psychology modality at PPL (Russia)
