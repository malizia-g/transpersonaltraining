## Biography

Stéphanie Larrue, PhD, is a psychotherapist, clinical counselling hypnotherapist and transpersonal supervisor specializing in transpersonal and integral psychotherapy. She has more than 25 years of experience in trance work practices, mindfulness, meditation and hypnotherapy, and has received extensive training in Bioenergetics and Biotransenergetics under the supervision of Dr. Pier Luigi Lattuada. Since 2010 she has been teaching at Saint Paul University in Canada, and her work focuses on holistic and psychospiritual healing, consciousness and transformative psychotherapy.
