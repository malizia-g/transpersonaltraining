## Biography

Nicolás Cambas uses playfulness, empathy and intuition as his primary tools. He left mainstream life at 21 following a shamanic experience, performing as a nomadic clown throughout Latin America for seven years and receiving shamanic initiations from indigenous communities including the Quechua, Cofán, Q'ero and Awá peoples, integrating their musical traditions and cosmovisions into his practice. He synthesized these experiences into CAMINOCREATIVO, a methodology offering creative, therapeutic and evolutive possibilities.

## Credentials

- Initiate in Andean-Amazonian Shamanism
- Performer and clown artist (7 years of nomadic practice across Latin America)
- Developer of the CAMINOCREATIVO methodology
- Studied performative arts under a range of teachers
