## Biography

Cathy Geils spent two decades working as a clinical psychologist across South African public hospitals, community clinics and private practice. She completed her training in constellations work in 2013 and blends hypnotherapy, eye-movement integration and constellation techniques within integral psychology. Her academic research employs organic inquiry methodology to investigate transpersonal psychology and consciousness transformation, with presentations and publications at international conferences.
