## Biography

Lydia Maidan is a specialist in early childhood development who leads psychological trainings for pregnant women and provides counseling to parents. She serves as a board member of the Association of Transpersonal Development in Moldova and coordinated the European Transpersonal Association Congress in Moldova in 2013.

## Credentials

- Certification in Introduction to Process-Oriented Psychology (Moscow)
- Training in Integrative Psychotechnologies, Art Therapy, Body-Oriented Therapy, Psychodrama
- Degree, State Pedagogical University, Department of Psychology and Special Psychopedagogy (Chișinău)
- Professional retraining, Institute of Integrative Psychology for Professional Development (Yaroslavl)
- Specialization, Moscow Institute of Gestalt and Psychodrama
