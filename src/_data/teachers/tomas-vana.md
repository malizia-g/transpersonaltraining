## Biography

Tomáš Váňa is a psychologist and director of the International Transpersonal Center HOLOS in the Czech Republic, where he coordinates clinical, educational and research activities in transpersonal psychology and psychotherapy. He is a certified Grof® Breathwork facilitator and a graduate of the Grof® Legacy Training, currently serving as an assistant facilitator in the European GLT-HOLOS training programme. His clinical work focuses on experiential psychotherapy, breathwork, non-ordinary states of consciousness, and the integration of contemporary clinical practice with transpersonal approaches.

Tomáš has presented at previous EUROTAS conferences, including Oxford (2024), where he shared research findings from the therapeutic programmes at HOLOS. He is also a co-organizer of the World Congress of Transpersonal Psychology 2027 in Prague, contributing to the international development of transpersonal psychology through clinical practice, education, research and professional collaboration.
