## Biography

Pier Luigi Lattuada leads EUROTAS and directs a Milan-based psychotherapy school fully recognized by the Italian Ministry of Education, University and Research since 2002. He founded Biotransenergetics and has published sixteen books and over 30 articles since the 1980s, presenting at more than 50 international conferences across Europe, Russia, the USA, Brazil and beyond. He is also scientific director of the Integral Transpersonal Journal and a past medical director of Lifegate Holistic Medicine Clinic.
