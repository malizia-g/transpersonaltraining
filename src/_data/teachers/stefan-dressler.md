## Biography

Stefan Dressler has run a private psychotherapy practice in Freiburg since 2005, using integrative, emotion-focused and process-experiential approaches drawing from psychodynamic, Gestalt and family reconciliation methodologies. He serves as both trainer and consultant for transpersonal psychology initiatives, including the two-year "Essence of Heart" training program he created, and has facilitated Holotropic Breathwork across multiple German cities and through international collaborations with institutions in China.

## Credentials

- Medical Doctor, specialization in psychiatry and psychotherapy
- Grof-certified Holotropic Breathwork Practitioner (2002)
- Professional Member of EUROTAS
- Teaching Therapist at ZIST (Munich, Germany)
