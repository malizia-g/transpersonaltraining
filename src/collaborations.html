Transpersonal work has always grown through relationship. Our school is part of a living web of institutes, training schools and teachers across Europe and beyond — colleagues who share standards, students and a common vision of psychotherapy as a path of consciousness. Here we honour that network: the partners we work with today, those we have collaborated with over the years, and the connections still to come.
