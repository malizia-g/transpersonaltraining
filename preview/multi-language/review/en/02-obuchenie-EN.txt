Training Philosophy
## Cultivating an Inner Space
This is more than just a professional training. It is for people whose purpose is to work with, support, and empower human beings.
It is an invitation to become a space for deeply profound transformation — beginning with yourself — through the pioneering approach of Transpersonal Psychotherapy.
The course follows an integrative approach, teaching therapeutic techniques, diagnostic and theoretical models, embodiment practices and healing nutrition. These constitute the building blocks of our training. In parallel to these, students will be cultivating an inner space to come from when working with clients.
EUROTAS-accredited, this transpersonal psychology degree is recognized across Europe and structured to meet international standards for transpersonal practice.

## Our story
### One school, East and West
The school was founded in 2021. It began with its Eastern programme (2021–2025), rooted in Moldova and Eastern Europe, and was followed by the Western programme (2024–2027).
For 2027–2030 the two sides come together. The online lectures are shared: one international community, learning side by side, taught by the same faculty from East and West. Lectures are delivered in English, with automatic translation available for Russian-speaking students.
The in-person intensives are held separately — in English for the Western track and in Russian for the Eastern track. Where a seminar is offered to both sides of the school, we provide translation. The two tracks are not sealed off from one another: with prior arrangement you are welcome to attend an intensive on the other side of the school.
Studied with us before? Everything from the earlier cycles — and the site you remember — is still online at old.transpersonal-training.com.
## The programme
### Program Overview
Our postgraduate program in transpersonal psychotherapy training represents a holistic approach to developing professional competence in transpersonal psychotherapy and Holotropic Breathing. This program is designed not only for mental health professionals, educators, and practitioners, but also for those interested in self-development, seeking to deepen their understanding of transpersonal psychology.
Transpersonal psychotherapy training integrates contemporary research in psychology with wisdom traditions, creating a comprehensive framework for understanding human consciousness, transformation, and healing. Our approach to holistic therapy acknowledges that self-development is not exclusively about symptomatic relief. Rather, it is about truly understanding what it means to be human, fully integrating all our humanity, a process of maturation from the wounded child so we can live from a space of freedom.

## Eligibility
### Who This Training Is For
#### General Eligibility (All Levels)
Applicants should demonstrate:
- Emotional stability and psychological readiness to engage in intensive experiential and reflective learning
- Capacity for self-reflection, personal responsibility, and relational maturity
- Openness to working with altered states of consciousness within a structured, ethical, and supervised context
- Willingness to engage in personal therapy and supervision as part of professional formation
- Master’s degree in a relevant field is not mandatory for the school’s certification. However, it might be required for EUROTAS certification or to practice psychotherapy in your country
Note: This training is experiential and may involve deep emotional and psychological processes. Applicants with acute, unmanaged psychiatric conditions that could be destabilized by non-ordinary states of consciousness may be advised to defer participation until clinically appropriate.
## Admissions
### How to Apply
Level 1 — Open Admission
Who Can Apply: Anyone interested in transpersonal psychology, consciousness exploration, and personal development. No formal prerequisites required.
#### Requirements
- Completed application form with personal statement
- Brief CV or professional background
- Psychological Biography
- Phone/video interview with program coordinator
- Confirmed availability to attend the required in-person intensives
Attendance. Each year asks for a minimum of 80% attendance: at least 4 in-person intensives and 80% of the online lectures. Students who fall below this may be asked to recuperate the missed lectures, at an additional cost, before qualifying for the next year. Where more has been missed, a second year of self-development may be the better path — something we discuss with the student, so that we agree together on what genuinely serves them.
Level 2 — Counseling Track
#### Requirements
- Level 1 completion documentation
- Personal statement describing professional goals
- Professional references (minimum 2)
- Interview with program coordinator
- Agreement to begin supervised practice hours under a Level 2 mentor
Level 3 — Psychotherapy Track
#### Requirements
- Level 1 and 2 completions
- Minimum 1-year clinical professional experience
- Documented transpersonal or consciousness-based work experience
- Clinical supervisor recommendation
- Detailed professional statement and clinical goals
- Comprehensive interview with faculty panel
- Agreement to the training’s ethical and confidentiality code for supervised clinical practice
## Commitment
### Your Commitment Structure
Our training asks for consistent engagement in learning, self-development, and supervised practice.
### Learning
- Weekly Online Lessons
    - 2 hours each week
    - 40 lessons per year (80 hours)
- Bi-Weekly Peer Group Meetings
    - Group discussion and homework integration
    - From Level 1: proposed practice for better outcomes
    - From Level 2: 20 meetings, usually around 2 hours each
- Interview Between Levels
    - Psychological and knowledge interview
    - Between Level 1 and Level 2
### Self-Development
- Therapy with a Transpersonal Therapist
    - Level 1: 10h
    - Level 2: 20h
    - Level 3: 20h
- Monthly Group Coaching
    - Transpersonal therapy group sessions, facilitated by a graduate of the school
    - Once a month, throughout each level
- Residential Trainings
    - 4 weekends per year (Thursday to Sunday)
    - 30 hours each = 120 hours/year = 480 hours in 4 years
- 150 hours of personal development (conferences, summer school, retreats)
- Holotropic Breathing practice — 8 to 10 sessions per year, scaled to each level’s length:
    - Level 1 (1 year): ~10 sessions
    - Level 2 (1.5 years): ~15 sessions
    - Level 3 (1.5 years): ~15 sessions
    - ≈ 40 sessions over the four years
### Supervision
School Track:
- Complete 50 hours of supervision for personal transpersonal psychotherapy practice with clients and/or supervision during Holotropic Breathing
    - 20 from Level 2
    - 30 from Level 3
    - Clients can be people from Level 1 (model clients) or clients from your personal practice.
EUROTAS Track:
- Complete 150 hours of supervision for personal transpersonal psychotherapy practice with clients
    - 50 from Level 2
    - 100 from Level 3 (or after Level 3)
- + 300 hours of clinical practice/internships (individual and group practice)
### Duration by Level
- 1st Level: 1 year — Self-Development
- 2nd Level: 1.5 years — Counselling Skills
- 3rd Level: 1.5 years — Psychotherapy* Skills
