## A profession of the mind, learned with the whole heart
If you want to become a transpersonal psychotherapist* and your purpose in life is to support and help other human beings, starting with yourself, this programme is for you. Our online Transpersonal Psychotherapy Training gives you the tools and skills to work in this pioneering field, integrating transpersonal counselling with Holotropic Breathing and somatic practice over a four-year course.
The training is a creative, innovative approach to psychotherapy and counselling, integrating developmental, relational and archetypal perspectives over a four year course.
Online lectures and intensive seminars in Europe · 2027–2030
Not ready for the full path? You can also join Level 1 on its own, as a stand alone year of self-development, with no obligation to continue.

A learning community with heart
This training is not only about methods. It is about becoming more present, more human, and more able to accompany others with depth and compassion.
