Core Teacher

Kati Wortelkamp

Head West

Anthropologist & Psychologist M.A.

Kati Wortelkamp is Co-Founder and Head West of the Transpersonal Training program, based near Freiburg, Germany. She brings decades of experience in Holotropic Breathwork® and transpersonal psychotherapy, overseeing the Western curriculum and ensuring the program meets EUROTAS standards for professional transpersonal training, and conducts training programs in Germany and Latvia. With over ten years at the Cancer-Hotline of the University Medical Center Freiburg, she brings deep compassion and practical expertise to therapeutic work, alongside an extensive background in bioenergetic, Kundalini and Iyengar yoga and other body-oriented methods.

  • Trainer in the Grof® Legacy Training programme, Germany
  • Innovation Trainer/Coach (motiv network, Institute of the German Economy, Federal Railroad of Switzerland)
  • Didactic therapy in Transpersonal Psychotherapy
  • One Light Healing Touch practitioner
  • Non-Medical Practitioner for Psychotherapy (Public Health Department Freiburg)
  • Person-Centered Psychotherapy training (University of Freiburg)

Private practice near Freiburg, offering Holotropic Breathwork® seminars for many years, alongside ten years at the Cancer-Hotline of the University Medical Center Freiburg.

Dr. Tina Lindhard

Spain

Tina Lindhard holds a doctorate in consciousness studies and specializes in transpersonal psychology with an emphasis on spirituality. She serves as a mentor for graduate students at IUPS and maintains a private practice blending meditation, craniosacral training and psychology, with a particular focus on trauma and grief work. An experienced educator, she has taught Arka Dhyana meditation internationally across Spain, Russia, France, Italy and the United States. Her scholarly work examines levels of consciousness, embryology, intuition, and the intersection of spirituality with mental health. She founded and chairs CCA Spain and sits on the board of CICA International, a consciousness research organization.

Guest

Rainer Pervöltz

Germany

Rainer Pervöltz studied German and French language and literature before relocating to California, where he trained in Gestalt and body psychotherapy with Jack Lee Rosenberg and Eric Marcus in Los Angeles, and later with Gerda Boyesen in London. He served as a trainer for the Biodynamic Institute during the 1980s–90s and co-founded the Chiron Centre in London in 1983. His spiritual education included work with Andrew DaPassano, Mantak Chia (Taoist Energy Training), Arnold Mindell (Dream Body) and Jaya Herbst (Psychic Healing). He now practices as a therapist, supervisor and teacher across Europe and co-directs the International Institute for Consciousness Exploration and Psychotherapy in Freiburg (IIBP).

  • UKCP (UK Council for Psychotherapy)
  • European Certificate of Psychotherapy (EAP)
  • Certificate of Accreditation, EABP (European Association for Body-Psychotherapy)
  • European Certificate of Transpersonal Psychotherapy (EUROTAS)

Switzerland / France

Guest

Jorge N. Ferrer, PhD

United States

Jorge N. Ferrer, PhD, is an internationally recognized scholar, psychologist, and author best known for developing the participatory approach to spirituality and consciousness studies. He is Associate Research Faculty at the California Institute for Human Science and served for more than two decades as a Professor of Psychology at the California Institute of Integral Studies, where he also chaired the Department of East-West Psychology. A former advisor to the United Nations-affiliated organization Religions for Peace and a member of the Esalen Institute’s Center for Theory and Research, Ferrer received the Fetzer Institute’s Presidential Award for his pioneering contributions to the study of human consciousness.

  • PhD, Psychology
  • Associate Research Faculty, California Institute for Human Science
  • Former Professor of Psychology & Chair, Department of East-West Psychology, California Institute of Integral Studies
  • Member, Esalen Institute’s Center for Theory and Research
  • Former advisor, Religions for Peace (United Nations-affiliated organization)
  • Recipient, Fetzer Institute Presidential Award

Award-winning author of several books, including Revisioning Transpersonal Theory, Participation and the Mystery, and Love and Freedom, with his forthcoming The Participatory Way: Reimagining Consciousness, Psychedelics, Embodiment, and Culture.

Extended faculty

Teachers Who Have Walked This Path With Us

Over the years, respected practitioners and scholars from around the world have generously shared their knowledge with our students — a living lineage that continues to shape how we teach today.

Lev Belogorodskii

Russia

Lev Belogorodskii is a psychotherapist trained in multiple modalities including Vivation, Biosynthesis, Biodynamic therapy, Family Constellations and Process Work under instructors such as David Boadella and Arnold Mindell. He has worked with individuals and groups in psychology, psychotherapy, coaching and business consulting for over 27 years.

Liudmila Serbina

Russia

Liudmila Serbina is a Ph.D. psychotherapist of the European Association of Psychotherapists (EAP) and Scientific Director of the Institute of Integrative Psychology and Psychotherapy training program. Practicing since 1995, she has studied Process Work directly under Arnold and Amy Mindell since 1999. She has conducted psychotherapeutic groups, authored seminars, and organized international conferences throughout Russia and the CIS.

Lilian Gscheidel

Germany

Lilian Gscheidel is part of the training team at Grof® Legacy Training Germany. She runs a private Gestalt therapy practice in Berlin, offers Grof® Breathwork weekends, and facilitates therapeutic aftercare groups for psychosomatic clinic patients, having lived in community for many years.

wandel-zart-und-wild.de

Vladimir Maykov

Russia

Recognized as the pioneer of transpersonal studies in Russia, Vladimir Maykov established over 15 training programs in transpersonal psychology and psychotherapy. He led an international project that produced roughly 80 transpersonal psychology texts in Russian, founded a video archive of over 500 free transpersonal resources, and directed three documentary films.

Pier Luigi Lattuada

Italy

Pier Luigi Lattuada leads EUROTAS and directs a Milan-based psychotherapy school fully recognized by the Italian Ministry of Education, University and Research since 2002. He founded Biotransenergetics and has published sixteen books and over 30 articles since the 1980s, presenting at more than 50 international conferences across Europe, Russia, the USA, Brazil and beyond. He is also scientific director of the Integral Transpersonal Journal and a past medical director of Lifegate Holistic Medicine Clinic.

Samvedam B. Randles

USA

Samvedam B. Randles directs the Inner Arts Institute in Watertown, Massachusetts, where she trains and teaches Breathwork, Systemic Constellation Work, and Trauma and Collective Trauma Integration Work. She has 20 years of breathwork instruction experience, founded the Center for Body-oriented Psychotherapy in Boston, and brings 18 years of constellation facilitation experience plus 15 years training facilitators in the modality.

Erik Andersen

South Africa

Erik Andersen is a teacher and facilitator specializing in esoteric studies and systemic healing, conducting training programs focused on how ancestral patterns influence participants’ lives. Living with muscular dystrophy, he channels this experience into his work, including an initiative called “Ramping Up” that combines practical action with systemic healing. He resides in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, with his family.

Cathy Geils

South Africa

Cathy Geils spent two decades working as a clinical psychologist across South African public hospitals, community clinics and private practice. She completed her training in constellations work in 2013 and blends hypnotherapy, eye-movement integration and constellation techniques within integral psychology. Her academic research employs organic inquiry methodology to investigate transpersonal psychology and consciousness transformation, with presentations and publications at international conferences.

Rita Aguila

Portugal

Rita Aguila brings a transpersonal approach and a specialization in trauma, with over 15 years exploring psychedelics therapeutically. She maintains a private practice serving teenagers and adults, facilitates women’s and couples’ retreats, and collaborates with Kosmicare at major festivals including BOOM.

Charlotte Kihl

Sweden

Charlotte Kihl transitioned mid-career to pursue psychosynthesis training, which has remained her foundational practice for over 20 years. She supervises fourth-year psychosynthesis students in Sweden and previously taught at coaching institutes. Her expertise spans individual and couples therapy, family work, supervision, mindfulness instruction and transpersonal programs through her Stockholm practice, Grow Beyond.

Dario Hampi Pacari

Italy

Dario Hampi Pacari is a psychologist, medicine man and vision quester who has spent two decades guiding sacred rituals and sharing the wisdom of indigenous traditions. He trained at the University of Rome La Sapienza and went on to work in Latin America alongside local communities, studying under medicine people in Ecuador and Guatemala — work that grew into humanitarian projects with marginalized youth there (2004–2008) and leadership roles supporting traditional healing knowledge across Europe and the Americas.

Dr. Kirsten E. Cameron

California, USA

Kirsten Cameron is a researcher, writer and educator whose work bridges transpersonal psychology, trauma studies and consciousness research. Her doctoral dissertation examined precognition as an adaptive response to childhood trauma. She has published in peer-reviewed journals including the International Journal of Transpersonal Studies and the Journal of Anomalous Experience and Cognition, and contributed to the Institute of Noetic Sciences. Her teaching emphasizes embodied awareness, intellectual curiosity, and the creative integration of science and spirit.

Ingrida Indane

Latvia

Ingrida Indane founded and directs the Transpersonal Education Institute in Latvia. She serves as executive director of the European Transpersonal Association and coordinates its Certification and Accreditation Committee. Her career spans art therapy, transpersonal psychology education and extensive international conference presentations, having conducted trainings in transpersonal arts therapy and psychology across Latvia and internationally since 1995.

Dr. Serge Obolensky Beddington-Behrens

England

Serge Beddington-Behrens is an Oxford-educated professional with forty years of experience conducting spiritual retreats worldwide. He co-founded the Institute for the Study of Conscious Evolution in San Francisco during the 1980s and authored “Awakening the Universal Heart” and “Gateways to the Soul” (2020), with “Amazing Grace” released in 2023. He divides his time between London and Mallorca.

Maria Kuehl-Weigmann

Germany

Maria Kuehl-Weigmann brought decades of clinical experience to the school through long-term work in student counseling at the Ruhr-University in Bochum and in her own practice, with specialized training across multiple therapeutic modalities. She spent four years working at Findhorn and remained closely involved with transpersonal training programs throughout her career. She passed away in 2023, remembered fondly as part of the school’s teaching history.

Lydia Maidan

Moldova

Lydia Maidan is a specialist in early childhood development who leads psychological trainings for pregnant women and provides counseling to parents. She serves as a board member of the Association of Transpersonal Development in Moldova and coordinated the European Transpersonal Association Congress in Moldova in 2013.

Cristina Revenco

Romania

Cristina Revenco practices as a licensed transpersonal psychotherapist with specialized experience working with children and adolescents facing developmental challenges, including autism spectrum disorders and ADHD. She founded Psycharmonia, a professional project, and regularly conducts webinars and workshops. Her training encompasses breathwork techniques, integral psychotherapy, art therapy, hypnosis and mindfulness practices, alongside broader interests in religious studies, mythology, philosophy and the arts.

Dennis Johnson

Austria

Dennis Johnson maintains an academic foundation in Tibetan and Buddhist Studies and has served as a librarian, translator, editor and interpreter. His professional experience includes roles at Windhorse Integral Mental Health Vienna and the Oxford Mindfulness Centre. His scholarly focus centers on transdisciplinary and transcultural research and practice, with applications toward individual, social and cultural transformation grounded in both traditional knowledge and modern science.

Vera Covaliciuc

Russia

Vera Covaliciuc holds advanced training in regressology and quantum healing hypnosis, specializing in past-life regression, reincarnation and karmic trauma healing. She brings nearly three decades of classical music performance experience across Russia and Europe, including international festival participation and competition awards. She hosts and produces the Regressology Podcast on Spotify and has translated philosophical works by Shrinivas Arka and scientific articles by Dr. Tina Lindhard on consciousness topics.
